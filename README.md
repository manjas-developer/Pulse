Pulse is a lightweight, privacy-focused social network and study communication platform built specifically for school friends and classmates. It allows students to exchange study questions, share homework files and audio notes, vote in campus polls, and chat one-on-one—without trial users, external ads, or algorithm tracking.
📱 Repository Structure
Everything in this project is lightweight and designed to be managed directly from any device (including an iPad or phone):
✨ Core Features
￼ Designed for iPad & Mobile Screens: Native smartphone bezel (￼) with live dynamic island notch, battery/Wi-Fi status, bottom navigation dock, and an instant full-screen toggle.
￼ Direct Device File Sharing: Pick photos, videos, homework documents, and PDFs directly from your iPad Files app, photo library, or phone storage—no external web links required.
￼ Live Voice Messages: Tap-to-record voice notes with live digital timers and animated waveform visualizers for both feed posts and 1-on-1 classmate chat.
￼ In-Browser Audio/Video Compression: Automatic canvas downsampling and audio resampling ensure media uploads sync fast without consuming heavy mobile data.
￼ School Channels & Study Hub: Filter pulses by study groups such as ⁠#HomeworkHelp⁠, ⁠#ScienceFair⁠, ⁠#SportsDay⁠, and ⁠#ArtClub⁠.
￼ Classroom Polls: Launch instant multiple-choice voting polls with real-time percentage indicators.
￼ Direct Friends Chat: Private student-to-student messaging drawer with attachment sharing and live sound feedback.
￼ Full Account Lifecycle: Safe student registration, customizable profile avatars, PIN protection, and full one-click account deletion.
🚀 Setting Up & Deploying via iPad
You do not need a computer or a terminal to set up or update this project. You can do everything in Safari on your iPad:
1. Open your repository in Safari on your iPad.
2. Press the ⁠.⁠ key (period) on your keyboard (or change ⁠github.com⁠ to ⁠github.dev⁠ in the URL). This opens GitHub's free web-based VS Code editor directly in Safari!
3. Paste or upload your ⁠index.html⁠, ⁠manifest.json⁠, and ⁠.github/workflows/deploy.yml⁠.
4. Tap Source Control on the left, type a commit message, and tap Commit & Push.
5. GitHub Actions will automatically deploy your live site to GitHub Pages in under 60 seconds!
(For full step-by-step screenshots and tips, see ipad_setup_guide.md.)
📲 Adding Pulse to Your iPad or iPhone Home Screen
1. Open your live GitHub Pages link in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button (the square icon with the upward arrow at the top right of Safari).
3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
4. Tap Add. Pulse will now appear on your iPad home screen like a native app with its custom school icon.

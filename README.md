# Pulse: School Friends Network 🎓📱

License: GPL v3  
Deploy to GitHub Pages  
PWA Ready

Pulse is a lightweight, privacy-focused social network and study communication platform built for school friends and classmates. It makes it easy for students to ask questions, share homework, coordinate on projects, and stay connected in a simple, mobile-first experience.

## ✨ Why Pulse?

Pulse is designed for real student life:

- Mobile-first and easy to use on iPad, iPhone, and other devices
- Built around privacy and simple communication
- Ideal for class discussions, study groups, and school communities
- Lightweight enough to manage directly from a phone or tablet

## 📱 Core Features

- Designed for iPad & Mobile Screens: Native smartphone-style layout with a dynamic island-inspired notch, battery/Wi-Fi status, bottom navigation dock, and instant full-screen mode.
- Direct Device File Sharing: Upload photos, videos, homework files, and PDFs directly from your iPad Files app, photo library, or phone storage—no external links required.
- Live Voice Messages: Record voice notes with live timers and animated waveform visualizers for both feed posts and 1-on-1 classmate chat.
- In-Browser Audio/Video Compression: Automatic downsampling keeps uploads fast and lightweight without eating up mobile data.
- School Channels & Study Hub: Explore channels like #HomeworkHelp, #ScienceFair, #SportsDay, and #ArtClub.
- Classroom Polls: Run instant multiple-choice polls with live percentage updates.
- Direct Friends Chat: Private messaging with attachments and sound feedback.
- Full Account Lifecycle: Safe registration, customizable profile avatars, PIN protection, and one-click account deletion.

## 📁 Repository Structure

Everything in this project is intentionally lightweight and designed to be managed directly from a mobile device, including an iPad or phone.

## 🚀 Setting Up & Deploying via iPad

You do not need a computer or a terminal to set up or update this project. You can do everything in Safari on your iPad:

1. Open your repository in Safari on your iPad.
2. Press the "." key on your keyboard, or change `github.com` to `github.dev` in the URL. This opens GitHub's free web-based VS Code editor directly in Safari.
3. Paste or upload your `index.html`, `manifest.json`, and `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` files.
4. Tap Source Control on the left, type a commit message, and tap Commit & Push.
5. GitHub Actions will automatically deploy your live site to GitHub Pages in under 60 seconds.

For full step-by-step screenshots and tips, see `ipad_setup_guide.md`.

## 📲 Adding Pulse to Your iPad or iPhone Home Screen

1. Open your live GitHub Pages link in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button in the top-right corner.
3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
4. Tap Add. Pulse will appear on your home screen like a native app.
